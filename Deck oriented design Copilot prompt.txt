My current thinking is that I want the game to be structured around the deck of cards. The deck will consist of 48 cards set up as a dictionary with the card number (1-48) as the key. Each card will be a unique entry with identifying information for suit and rank symbol, both by a two character label and full name. . Rank will also be included as numeric for card comparison when evaluating trick winner. The image file location will be included. The card value for counting will be included, Within the card entry will be a state field that will define a cards current role in a round.  This state filed will identify where on the GUI the card is currently positioned. Values will include deck, hand1, hand2, trick, meld1, meld2, stock, trump designator, won1, won2. The deck does not have a GUI mapping. Player1 and Player2 also do not appear on the GUI. There will be lists corresponding to hand1, hand2, meld1, meld2, stock, trump designator, won1, and won2. The lists will consist of card numbers which will be used to accessed the card definitons in the deck for information as required. The deck cards will not "move" around, just their indices. There will be GUI PyQt5 labels that define locations for each of the states. The display of the cards on the playing table  will be generated dynamically with respect to the location of the PyQt5 lables. There will be a GUI control panel for the control the change in the state of the card indices from hand1, hand2 to trick area, meld1, meld2 and won1, won2 areas. I currently want to implement just a minimum viable product form that consists of just one hand through the flow. Not every thing at once.
